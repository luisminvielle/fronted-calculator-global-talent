Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about global talent hiring and cost calculations

How accurate are these cost calculations?

+
Our calculations are based on extensive market research and average employment costs across different countries. However, actual costs can vary based on specific job roles, experience levels, local regulations, and negotiation terms. We recommend using these figures as estimates and consulting with local HR experts for precise calculations.

Which countries are supported?

+
We currently support 12 countries including Brazil, Philippines, Kosovo, Albania, Norway, Sweden, Germany, France, Ireland, Colombia, Mexico, and the United States. We're continuously expanding our coverage to include more countries based on market demand.

What costs are included in the calculation?

+
Our calculations include base salary, taxes and social contributions, benefits and insurance, and administrative overhead. This provides a comprehensive view of the total employment cost beyond just the salary figure.

How often are the cost figures updated?

+
We update our cost data quarterly to reflect changes in tax rates, benefit requirements, and market conditions. Currency exchange rates are updated in real-time to ensure accuracy.

Can I hire talent from these countries directly?

+
Yes, our platform facilitates direct hiring from all supported countries. We handle the legal, compliance, and administrative aspects of international employment, making it seamless for companies to build global teams.

What are the benefits of hiring global talent?

+
Global hiring offers access to diverse skill sets, cost advantages in certain markets, 24/7 development cycles, and unique perspectives that drive innovation. Many companies find that international teams bring valuable cultural diversity and specialized expertise.
